What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Useful entries record the source link, selected variant, listed price, size information, and the date checked. Treat the list as a starting point for research: it does not guarantee product quality, authenticity, availability, or delivery.

Fit & construction

Compare what you can measure.

For shoes, compare the seller’s size chart with the measurements of a pair that already fits. For clothing, use a familiar garment as your reference rather than relying only on letter sizes.

  • Sneakers: size chart, width information, outsole views, and visible stitching.
  • Hoodies and T-shirts: chest width, garment length, shoulder width, and fabric details.
  • Jackets and sets: layering room, sleeve length, closures, and the pieces included.
  • Headwear and jewelry: circumference, adjustability, dimensions, and material information.
Budget & delivery

The item price is one part.

A low listed price can become less attractive once delivery and other charges are included. Compare estimates using the same destination and shipping assumptions.

Bulky shoes, padded jackets, and protective packaging may affect shipping quotes. Use the current parcel weight and dimensions when those details become available.

Kakobuy QC guide

What photos can tell you.

Quality-control photos can help you assess visible condition, color, measurements, and whether the item appears to match your selected option. Lighting and camera angles can change how an item looks.

For shoes, request clear side, heel, toe, and outsole views when needed. For garments, flat measurements and close views of stitching can be more useful than another distant photo.

Photos alone do not establish authenticity, material composition, or long-term durability. Verify those claims separately when they matter to your decision.

Brand references

Keep style and verification separate.

Nike sneakers, Louis Vuitton apparel, Gucci headwear, and Hermès jewelry appear here as fashion references in original illustrations. These illustrations do not represent verified listings or available inventory.

When researching branded products, check the exact item description, seller information, provenance, and purchase terms. Brand names or logos in a listing are not proof of authenticity.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide that links to CAFANS categories. It is not an official Kakobuy service and does not claim affiliation with the fashion brands mentioned.

How do I compare Kakobuy finds?

Compare the exact product variant, material description, measurements, seller photos, available QC evidence, and estimated total cost. Record when each detail was checked because listings and prices can change.

Do QC photos prove a product is authentic?

No. Quality-control photos can help assess visible condition, measurements, color, and whether the selected item appears correct. They do not by themselves establish authenticity, material composition, or long-term durability.

How should I estimate the total shopping cost?

Start with the item price, then account for domestic delivery, any service charges, international shipping, and applicable taxes or duties. Use current quotes for the destination, parcel dimensions, weight, and shipping method.
